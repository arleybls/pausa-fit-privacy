Privacy Policy

JeJ — The Fasting Companion (Android app, package com.arleybls.jej)
Effective date: 3 September 2026

JeJ stores everything on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no third parties.

What the app stores — on your device only

  • Your fasting history: start and end times, goal durations, the time zone a fast started in, and any optional mood rating or note you add.
  • Your settings: language, appearance, color scheme, ring style, fasting schedule, reminder preferences, and notification choices.

All of this lives in a local database and local settings storage inside the app's private area on your phone. It is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No collection or transmission of personal data.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party services receive any data from the app.
  • No network requests. The app requests the standard network permission because its underlying framework includes it, but the app itself never contacts a server.

Notifications

Reminders and phase notifications are scheduled locally on your device by the app. No notification service outside your phone is involved. You can choose reduced notification detail in the app's privacy settings, and you can disable notifications entirely in your system settings.

Export and import

You can export your data as a file at any time. The export is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it. Importing reads a file you select. The app never sends these files anywhere on its own.

Deleting your data

The app includes a "Delete all data" option that permanently erases every fast, setting, and reminder on the device. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data.

External links

The app contains a link to an external support resource (the National Eating Disorders Association website). Tapping it opens your browser; the website you visit has its own privacy policy.

Children

JeJ is designed for adults and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
